What happened in the real example?

We asked whether a west-facing room could stay comfortable without air conditioning. Across 5 actual DeepSeek requests (8,990 tokens), an audit identified an experiment proposal with overstated source attribution and requested room data and a decision about including shading. No physical experiment was performed; no judgment change was adopted.

The sources were checked and supplied by the operator. The case demonstrates a useful limit being identified, not proof of cooling performance or a guarantee that models cannot make mistakes.
